Retinal Vessel Segmentation – OHSU

A pipeline that takes Doppler OCT scans, segments each retinal vessel's wall and lumen, and measures the wall-to-lumen ratio in µm, a non-invasive way to flag cardiovascular risk.

  • 0.89 mean Dice on 10 held-out test scans the model never saw (0.96 in training)
  • Hand-annotated 300+ vessels across 30 scans with a grading protocol I wrote
  • Two nnU-Net v2 models on dual-channel input (Doppler phase shift plus log reflectance)
